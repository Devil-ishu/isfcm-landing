Corporate training in Chandigarh and the Tricity

Leadership presence, influence and communication training for teams in Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and across India. Built around live demonstration and practice, not slides.

Request a diagnosis Ways to work together

Programmes

  • Leadership presence for new and mid-level managers: running meetings, handling pushback, being heard without raising your voice.
  • Influence and negotiation for sales and client-facing teams: the silent objection, reading hesitation, holding price.
  • Recognising manipulation at work: pressure tactics, guilt and frame control, and how to respond calmly.
  • Voice and presence for leaders who present to boards, clients and large teams.

Formats

Talks of 60 to 90 minutes for any audience size. Half-day workshops for up to 40. One- and two-day intensives for leadership teams. Multi-session series for a fixed cohort, with practice between sessions.

How it is arranged

A short call about who is in the room and what they need to do differently. The session is built for that audience, delivered in person or online, and followed by a short note on what to reinforce.
