Refund and cancellation policy

ISFCM LLP · Last updated 3 November 2026

Diagnosis call

  • If it doesn't help: if the call doesn't shift anything for you, tell us within 7 days of the call and you'll get a full refund. No forms, no argument.
  • Cancelling before the call: cancel at least 12 hours before your slot for a full refund, or reschedule instead.
  • Missed calls: a call missed without notice is not refunded, but we will offer you one new slot.

Mentorship and programmes

  • Before your first session: full refund if you cancel.
  • After your first session: if you decide not to continue, tell us before your second session and we refund the fee minus the cost of the session delivered, worked out pro rata.
  • After that: fees are not refundable, because sessions and preparation have been committed to you. You may pause once, for up to 30 days, and resume later.
  • If we end a programme for reasons on our side, we refund the sessions not yet delivered.
  • Deposits and instalments: the same rules apply to the amount paid so far.

Seminars and workshops

Cancellation terms are set in the written agreement with your institution. If none is agreed, cancellations made 14 days or more before the date are refunded in full, and later cancellations can be moved to a new date instead.

ISTC coaching

Full refund if you cancel before the batch starts. After the first week of classes, fees are not refundable.

How to ask for a refund

Email gaurangmisra@isfcm.com or WhatsApp +91 94641 51610 with your name, the service and your payment reference. We confirm within 3 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
