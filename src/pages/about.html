How this started

I didn't set out to study voice. I started making reels for my businesses, and the camera showed me something I couldn't hear in person: I sounded young. Not wrong. Just light.

The obvious fix was the one every speaking coach sells: drop your voice, sound deeper, sound like authority. I tried it. It cost me the one thing that mattered. People stopped relating to me.

So I did the harder thing. Over twelve months I learned to read the person in front of me and tune my tone to the way they listen. Today I don't have a deep voice, a sharp voice and a normal voice. I have one voice that adjusts to every person I speak to.

That adjustment is what I teach.

What I believe

Your words are the least important part of what you say. I make mistakes when I speak. Most people never notice, because they are following my tone and what I'm trying to convey, not my grammar.

People hear everything in their own manner, not yours. So influence isn't polish. It's understanding the other person's mind, then speaking so they take in your point their way.

That is why I don't teach full sentences, fancy vocabulary or a deeper voice. Those make you sound rehearsed. What works is control: knowing the effect you want, reading the person, and adjusting as you go.
