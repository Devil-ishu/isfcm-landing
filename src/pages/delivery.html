Delivery policy

ISFCM LLP · Last updated 3 November 2026

ISFCM LLP sells services, not physical products. Nothing is shipped.

How services are delivered

  • Diagnosis call: after payment you are taken straight to a booking page to choose your slot. The video call link arrives by email and WhatsApp, and the call takes place at the time you choose, usually within 7 days of payment.
  • Mentorship and programmes: sessions are delivered one to one over video on a schedule agreed on your first call, normally starting within 7 days of payment.
  • Seminars and workshops: delivered at your institution or online on the agreed date.
  • ISTC coaching: delivered online or at our office in Sector 31D, Chandigarh, from the batch start date.
