Seniority changes the problem.

Early in a career you're judged on what you know. At the top, everyone knows enough. You're judged on whether people follow you.

  • Fewer people tell you the truth

    Your team agrees with you. Your board is polite. Feedback stops arriving exactly when it matters most.

  • Every room costs more

    A wavering minute in a fundraise, a board review or a negotiation is priced in lakhs and crores.

  • Your presence becomes the culture

    How you sound under pressure is how your company behaves under pressure.

How the programme runs

  1. Before

    Selection call

    A private conversation about your role, the rooms ahead and what isn't working. If the programme isn't the right fit, you'll hear that directly.

  2. Month 1

    Training

    Several sessions a week. Your voice, presence, frame and reading of people, rebuilt around the specific meetings in your calendar.

  3. Months 2 to 7

    Conditioning

    Preparation before high-stakes rooms, debriefs after them, adjustments in between. Until it holds without thinking.
