Seniority changes the problem

At your level nobody tells you how you come across. Rooms are politer and decisions quieter, so you find out late, if at all.

  • Your voice isn't heard the way you intend

    You make the right call and it's received as an opinion.

  • Decisions drift after you leave the room

    Agreement in the meeting, a different outcome a week later.

  • The next level needs a different presence

    What got you here was competence. What gets you further is how you're read.

How the programme runs

  1. 1

    Selection call

    A private conversation to decide whether this is the right work for you. Not everyone is accepted.

  2. 2

    One month of training

    Intensive one-to-one sessions on voice, presence, reading people and articulation.

  3. 3

    Six months of conditioning

    A session every two weeks, built around your real meetings, pitches and negotiations.

What changes

  • You speak at the right moment

    And say less, with more weight.

  • You hold the room in disagreement

    Pushback stops moving you off your position.

  • You present to boards and investors with ease

    Because you're reading them while you speak.

  • You're understood in their terms

    Your point lands the way you meant it, with every kind of listener.
