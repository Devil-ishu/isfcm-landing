Adviser to founders, senior professionals and their teams on influence, executive presence and voice. Founder of ISFCM LLP, Chandigarh. More than 500 one-to-one cases since he began this work.

The work

Most of Gaurang's clients are already good at what they do. They come because something isn't converting: the investor who goes quiet, the promotion that goes to someone louder, the negotiation where the terms moved the moment they hesitated.

His view is that this is rarely a skills problem. It's a perception problem. People decide whether to follow someone in the first few seconds, from tone, timing and stillness, long before they weigh the argument. That layer can be read, and it can be trained.

He works one to one, privately, and only with people he believes he can move.

How he works

  • He argues with you

    The first sessions are debate, not instruction. Every assumption you hold about how you should come across gets tested, until what's left is what you actually want.

  • Everything is built for one person

    No batch curriculum. Each programme is designed around the specific rooms a client needs to win.

  • You apply it between sessions

    Learn, use it in a real meeting, report back, adjust. The feedback from real rooms decides what comes next.

  • Training, then conditioning

    Knowing what to do isn't the goal. The goal is doing it under pressure without thinking about it.

What he believes

Your words are the least important part of what you say. People follow tone and intent, not grammar. Polished sentences and a deliberately deep voice make you sound rehearsed.

Everyone hears you through their own frame. Influence is understanding that frame, then putting your point across so they take it in as their own.

He learned this the hard way, by retraining his own voice after the standard advice to "sound deeper" made people stop relating to him. The voice work came out of that.
