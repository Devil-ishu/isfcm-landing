The Voice Audit

A 30-minute diagnosis of what your voice is costing you.

  1. You apply and send 2 minutes of yourself speaking.A pitch, a team address, or simply you explaining your business. A phone recording is fine.
  2. I break your recording down before the call.Pace, pitch, pauses, fillers, and how your authority holds under pressure.
  3. On the call, I play it back and show you where you lose the room.Moment by moment, in your own voice.
