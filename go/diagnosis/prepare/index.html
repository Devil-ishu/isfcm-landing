How To Show Up On The Call

This is a diagnostic, not a conversation. I ask, you answer.

  • 1Quiet room, camera on, earphones in. If you can't speak freely, you won't.
  • 2Join two minutes early. The link is in your booking confirmation.
  • 3Don't rehearse. The pattern shows in how you answer, not what you prepared.
  • 4Be blunt with yourself. I can only reverse what you let me see. The call is recorded for my review only, and nothing is shared.
By minute 45, one of two things will be true: you'll know the exact mechanism running you and how to reverse it yourself — or you'll know it runs deeper and needs structured work. Either way, you'll know exactly where you stand.
