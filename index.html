One of these is usually running you.

Capable people rarely fail on knowledge. They fail on one of a handful of patterns they can't see from the inside.

  • The three-second verdict

    You're placed in a status bracket before you finish your first sentence. Almost none of it is what you said.

  • The silent objection

    The reason someone gives for saying no is rarely the real one. The real one ends the deal without being spoken.

  • The convincing trap

    The harder you argue for your value, the less of it they see. Effort reads as need.

  • The approval leak

    Something in how you speak signals that you need their yes. The moment it registers, your position weakens.

  • The flinch

    Every pushback is a test of whether you hold. Your voice answers before you do.

  • The blank

    Your mind empties at exactly the moment it counts. Not nerves: a specific threat response with a specific fix.

Ways to work together

  • Reading people
