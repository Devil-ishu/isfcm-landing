The Pre-Call Frame

This is a diagnostic, not a conversation. I ask, you answer. How you show up decides how deep we get in 45 minutes.

  • 1Quiet room, camera on, earphones in. No driving, no office floor, no one listening in. If you can't speak freely, you won't.
  • 2Join two minutes early. The link is in your booking confirmation. The call starts on time and ends on time.
  • 3Bring one real situation. The one you'll describe in the questionnaire below. One is enough — the pattern surfaces fast.
  • 4Don't rehearse your answers. The pattern shows in how you answer, not what you prepared. Polished answers hide it.
  • 5Be blunt with yourself. I can only reverse what you let me see. Nothing you say is recorded, shared or judged.

By minute 45, one of two things will be true: you'll know the exact mechanism running you and how to reverse it yourself — or you'll know it runs deeper and needs structured work. Either way, you'll know exactly where you stand.
