One-to-one mentorship.

Authority Conditioning, trained around you: your voice, your rooms, the people you need to move. By application, after a diagnosis.

What we work on

Every programme is built from what the diagnosis finds. These are the areas it draws from.

  • Voice and Tonal Authority

    Pace, pitch, pauses and weight. Sounding certain without sounding rehearsed.

  • Body language

    Posture, stillness and the signals you send before you speak.

  • Reading people

    Noticing what someone needs to hear, and the moment they're ready to hear it.

  • Power dynamics

    How status moves in a room, and how to hold your position under pushback.

  • Mind conditioning

    Staying composed when the stakes rise, so the training holds in the real room.

How it runs

  1. 1

    Diagnosis

    We find the pattern and agree what the work needs to change.

  2. 2

    Training

    About 30 one-to-one sessions over two to three months, two or three a week, with practice between them.

  3. 3

    Conditioning

    Real rooms, reviewed together, until the new way of speaking is the default.

The fee depends on what the work involves and is shared after your diagnosis. I don't take everyone. If a smaller piece of work will solve it, that's what I'll recommend.
