The full method, one to one.

Sixty to ninety days of private work on how people actually decide: identity, body language and voice, dark psychology, and the dynamics of attraction in rooms and relationships. Every session is designed for one person. You.

Apply for mentorship The four phases

Four phases

  1. Phase 1

    Identity

    Society trained you to show the median version of yourself: likeable, reliable, easy to place, and easy to ignore. We argue through every belief you hold about how you should come across, until what's left is what you actually want. The clutter goes. Focus and decisiveness come back.

  2. Phase 2

    Body language and vocal mastery

    How to walk, sit and enter a room. How to read the smallest shifts in someone's voice and posture. Pace, pitch and pauses, so your voice carries authority without strain, and you can move between warm and commanding on purpose.

  3. Phase 3

    Dark psychology

    The mechanics most people only feel. Frame control. Conditioning. Storytelling as leverage. How manipulators actually operate: the subtle ones who leave you feeling vaguely "off", and patterns like DARVO (deny, attack, reverse victim and offender). How to recognise it while it's happening, defend, and take the frame back.

  4. Phase 4

    Social and corporate seduction

    Not pickup. Being the person a room gravitates to. Earning favour in meetings, being remembered after an event, building curiosity instead of chasing attention. Your voice is heard and counted every time a decision is made.

How it runs

  • Length60 to 90 days, about 30 sessions. It ends when the change holds, not on a date.
  • RhythmTwo or three sessions a week, 45 to 60 minutes each, over video
  • MethodLearn it, use it in a real conversation, report back, adjust. Real rooms are the training ground.
  • TravelAway for a week? You get drills to practise and we pick up when you're back.
  • PrivacySessions are recorded for your progress only. Never shared, never published.
  • FeeShared after your diagnosis, with a no-cost EMI option

What changes, and when

  1. First 10 sessions

    A clearer mind

    You stop spending energy on what doesn't matter. Overthinking turns into deciding.

  2. The middle

    You start reading people

    You notice what others miss: the hesitation, the test, the real objection.

  3. The end

    Control without effort

    You can't influence every decision, but you can predict most of them. That's where calm comes from.

A note on dark psychology

It isn't a branch of academic psychology. It's a name for what drives behaviour when people aren't being fully honest, including with themselves. These mechanisms already act on you every day.

Learning them does two things: you stop being moved by people who use them on you, and you understand influence well enough to use it cleanly. If your goal is to harm or deceive someone, this programme isn't for you, and the diagnosis will show that.
