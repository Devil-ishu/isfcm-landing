Before your audit

Five minutes of prep makes this call twice as useful.

1. Has your recording reached me?

If you haven't sent your 2-minute voice note yet, send it now. I need it at least 12 hours before the call.

Send My Recording on WhatsApp →

2. Set up for the call

  • A quiet room. Your car or a café won't work.
  • Laptop if possible, with headphones. I'll play parts of your recording back to you.
  • Join 2 minutes early. The link is in your confirmation email.
  • Have one real situation in mind: the meeting or pitch where your voice matters most.

3. Know how this ends

There are only two outcomes, and I'll be direct about which one it is:

  • You're a fit. I'll show you what the program looks like for you, and you decide.
  • You're not ready yet. I'll tell you plainly, and you leave with a clear diagnosis to work on yourself.

Either way, you'll know exactly what your voice is costing you.
