Privacy policy

ISFCM LLP · Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data ISFCM LLP ("ISFCM", "we", "us") collects when you use this website, book or attend a session with Gaurang Misra, or contact us, and what we do with it. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, city, role, company or institution, and anything you write in a form, application or questionnaire.
  • Session information: notes and recordings of your sessions, and your answers to the pre-call questionnaire.
  • Payment information: the amount, order and payment reference. Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical information: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and the advert or link that brought you here, collected through cookies and similar tools.

2. Why we use it

  • To book, deliver and follow up on your diagnosis, mentorship, programme, seminar or coaching batch.
  • To prepare for your sessions and track your progress.
  • To reply to enquiries and applications.
  • To process payments, refunds and invoices, and to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • To send reminders and updates about services you asked for. You can opt out of non-essential messages at any time.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

3. Session recordings

Sessions are recorded so that Gaurang can review your progress and prepare for the next session. Recordings and session notes are confidential. They are never published, used in marketing or shared with anyone outside ISFCM without your written permission. Tell us at the start of a session if you would prefer it not to be recorded.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share only what is needed with the services that help us run the business:

  • Razorpay, for payments
  • Cal.id, for booking calls
  • Google (Workspace, Sheets and Analytics), for records, email and website measurement
  • Meta, for measuring and improving our adverts
  • Fathom and Google Meet, for video sessions and their recordings
  • WhatsApp, for messages you choose to exchange with us
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

5. Cookies

This website uses cookies from Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand visits and measure our adverts. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as you are in touch with us and for up to three years afterwards. Session recordings are kept while you are working with us and deleted within twelve months of your last session, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose, and you can withdraw consent you have given. Write to gaurangmisra@isfcm.com. We reply within 30 days.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and trusted service providers to protect your data. No method of storing or sending data online is completely secure, but we work to keep the risk as low as we reasonably can.

9. Children

Our consulting services are for adults. Students under 18 may join ISTC coaching only with the consent of a parent or guardian, who can contact us about the student's data at any time.
