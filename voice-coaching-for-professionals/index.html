Voice coaching for professionals

If you sound unsure, rushed or younger than you are, people discount what you say, however good it is. Voice coaching fixes how you sound in meetings, pitches and interviews without turning you into someone else.

Request a diagnosis Ways to work together

The usual mistakes

  • Pitch rising at the end of sentences, so decisions sound like questions
  • Speaking too fast under pressure, which reads as junior and invites interruption
  • Filling every silence with filler words
  • Forcing a deeper voice, which sounds performed and makes you harder to relate to

What changes

You learn to set your pace deliberately: roughly 80 to 90 beats per minute for authority, slower for trust, never the 100-plus that nerves choose. You learn to use falling tones to decide, pauses to let people agree, and emphasis on the one word that matters. Then you learn to adjust all of it to the listener in front of you.

Questions

Will I sound fake?

No. The point is the opposite of putting on a voice. You train a few habits until they are automatic, so the result sounds like you on your best day.

How long does voice coaching take?

Most people hear a clear difference within a few weeks of two to three sessions a week, then condition it until it holds under pressure.

Do you work on accents?

The work is on authority and clarity, not on removing an accent. A neutral accent delivered hesitantly still loses the room.
