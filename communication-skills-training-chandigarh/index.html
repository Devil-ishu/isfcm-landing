Not another personality development class

Most courses teach vocabulary, grooming and scripted introductions. Those help on paper. In a real interview, client meeting or negotiation, people respond to how certain you sound, how still you are and whether you read them correctly. That is what this training works on.

What you can train

  • Public speaking and presentations without sounding rehearsed
  • Interview and group discussion performance
  • Voice: pace, pitch and pauses
  • Body language and first impressions
  • Influence, negotiation and handling pushback
  • Leadership presence for new managers

Formats

Private one-to-one coaching, online or at the Sector 31D office. Workshops and seminars for colleges and companies across the Tricity. Every engagement starts with a short conversation about what you, or your group, need to be able to do.
