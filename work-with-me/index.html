What comes after depends on what I find. Some people need one conversation. Some need months of work. A few aren't ready yet, and I'll tell them so.

The method: Authority Conditioning

Training first, so you understand what's happening in a room. Then conditioning, so it holds when the stakes are real and you don't have time to think.

  • Tonal Authority

    Your voice, tuned to the way each listener hears. Pace, pitch, pauses and weight, without sounding like someone else.

  • Reading the room

    The small signals in a face, a posture or a silence that tell you what a person needs to hear, and when.

  • Presence and body language

    How you enter, sit, pause and hold attention before you say a word.

  • Articulation in their terms

    Putting your thinking across so the other person understands it as their own idea.

Is this for you?

It is, if

  • You're capable, but your voice isn't carrying the weight it should in the room
  • You lose decisions you should be winning, often at the last minute
  • You're moving into a bigger role and the rooms are getting harder
  • You're willing to practise between sessions

It isn't, if

  • You want a script to read out
  • You want motivation rather than work
  • You want to deceive the people you speak to
  • You're looking for therapy. This is about how you're received, not your past

Questions

Is this online or in person?

Sessions run one to one over video, so you can work from anywhere, two or three times a week.

How is this different from a speaking or soft-skills course?

Those teach you to sound polished. I teach you to be understood the way you mean it, which often means speaking less, pausing more and reading the person in front of you. No scripts, no vocabulary drills, no "speak in a deeper voice".

Why can't I buy mentorship directly?

Because I don't know yet whether you need it. The diagnosis tells us both. If a smaller piece of work will solve it, that's what I'll recommend.

What does it cost?

The diagnosis is pay what you believe it's worth, from ₹499. Fees for mentorship and the executive programme are shared on the call, once we both know what the work involves.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Client names never appear on this site or anywhere else. Sessions are recorded so I can review your progress; recordings are never shared or published. You can ask for any session not to be recorded.
