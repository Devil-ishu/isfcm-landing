Executive presence coaching

Executive presence is the judgement people make, in seconds, about whether you're someone to follow. It decides who gets the promotion, whose number the board believes and whose idea survives the meeting. It can be trained.

Request a diagnosis Ways to work together

What executive presence actually is

Most definitions are vague: gravitas, confidence, charisma. In practice it comes down to a few things people can observe: how certain your voice sounds, how still you are under pressure, how you hold your position when challenged, and whether you read the room before you speak.

None of that is personality. All of it is behaviour, and behaviour can be changed with the right feedback and enough repetition.

What we work on

  • Voice. Pace, pitch and silence. Statements that land as decisions, not requests.
  • Stillness. Removing the fidgeting, nodding and over-explaining that read as need.
  • Frame. Holding your position under pushback without raising your voice.
  • Reading the room. Knowing what each person needs to hear, and when they're about to say no.
  • High-stakes rehearsal. Board reviews, investor calls and negotiations prepared in advance and debriefed after.

Who it is for

Founders raising or running a growing team. CXOs and partners stepping into bigger rooms. Senior professionals who are good at the work and keep losing decisions to someone louder. Sessions are one to one, online, and confidential.

Questions

How long does executive presence coaching take?

Most clients work over 60 to 90 days in one-to-one mentorship. Founders and CXOs can take the executive programme: one month of training followed by six months of conditioning.

Is this online or in person?

Sessions run online over video, so you can work from anywhere in India or abroad. In-person sessions are possible in Chandigarh.
